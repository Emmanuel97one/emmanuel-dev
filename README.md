## 🐔 AVIDATA PRO

### Poultry Farm Management Software

**AVIDATA PRO** is a poultry farm management system designed to help poultry businesses organize farm operations, production records, inventory, finance, reporting, and management activities.

It supports structured management of poultry operations and provides tools for monitoring business and farm performance.

### Core Capabilities

- Batch and flock management
- Daily farm records
- Inventory and stock management
- Financial management
- Supplier and customer management
- Labor and payroll management in supported editions
- Asset and maintenance management
- Reporting and analytics
- Farm performance monitoring
- Planning and forecasting
- Multi-farm capabilities in supported editions
- Global management dashboard in supported editions
